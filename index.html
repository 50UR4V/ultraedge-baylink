BAYLINK FLASHER

Flash your Baylink.

Put the UltraEdge firmware on your Baylink. No sign-in.

Before you start

  • The Baylink works with the UltraEdge app on an Android phone.
  • Check your radio is on the supported radios list.
  • You need an ESP32-C3 board with 4 MB flash and a USB data cable.
  • Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, or Chrome on an Android phone.

This browser can't reach USB devices

Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS), or in Chrome on an Android phone with a USB-C cable or adapter.

1 Where will this module go?

Pick the right one before you flash.

2 Firmware

Loading the firmware list…

3 Connect and flash

  1. Plug the ESP32-C3 board into this computer or phone by USB.
  2. Press Connect and pick the board in the list (often "USB JTAG/serial debug unit" or "ESP32-C3").
  3. Press Flash. Keep the cable in until it says done (about a minute).
Advanced

Only for a board that won't start after a normal flash. It also forgets the module's paired phones and its registration (you register again in the app).

Something went wrong

Done

  1. Unplug the board and build it into its case.
  2. Fit it to the radio and set up the link in the app: see the wireless chapter of the manual.
  3. Registration comes later, in the app over Bluetooth. You won't need to open the case.

Update a Baylink you already have

No need to open the case. Download the file for your module's kind on your phone, then in the app open Wireless module › Update module firmware and pick it. The app and the module check the file before anything changes.
